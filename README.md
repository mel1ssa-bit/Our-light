# Our Light

Cooperative local split-screen game in the browser. Player 1 lights the forest; Player 2 fights ghosts — but only what the light reveals.

## Play

Open **`index.html`** in your browser, or double-click **`ABRIR-JOGO.bat`**.

## Controls

| Player | Role | Keyboard | Controller |
|--------|------|----------|------------|
| P1 | Light | A/D choose · W confirm · WASD move · mouse aim | Left stick move · right stick aim · A confirm |
| P2 | Sword | ←/→ choose · Space confirm · arrows move · Space attack | Left stick · A attack |

Pair a Bluetooth/USB controller in Windows, then press any button in the game.

## Stages

1. **Bloom Grove** — petals & spring moss  
2. **Emerald Path** — deep green woods  
3. **Rose Dusk** — sunset meadow  

Defeat all 5 ghosts in each stage to advance. Shared HP — protect each other.
